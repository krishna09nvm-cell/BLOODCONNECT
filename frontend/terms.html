Terms and Conditions

  1. LifeDrop helps people find blood donation camps, blood banks and donors. It is not a medical service. In an emergency call 108 or 112.
  2. Blood banks and hospitals come from the official National Health Portal directory. Always call before you go: blood stock is not shown.
  3. Camps are shown only after verification by an administrator. Organisers must give true details and be allowed to publish the camp.
  4. Sample (demo) data is labelled and is not real.
  5. Donor eligibility is decided by the blood bank's doctor, not by this website.
  6. Do not misuse other people's contact details.

Privacy Policy

  • What we store: name, date of birth, email, mobile number, gender (optional), state, city, PIN code and role.
  • Passwords are never stored. Only a secure one-way hash (scrypt) is saved.
  • Who can see it: only you, in your profile. Your email address and mobile number are never shown publicly.
  • Camps: the camp contact details you enter are shown publicly only after an administrator has checked them.
  • Login: a secure cookie keeps you logged in; "Remember me" keeps it for 30 days.
  • Your location is used only in your browser to find nearby camps and is not saved.
